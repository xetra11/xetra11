## ⚡ Donnervolk Online

> **Lead your kin through the generations, in a world where every rumour travels.**

**Donnervolk Online** is a slow, asynchronous online strategy and dynasty game set in Iron Age Germania during the Roman Imperial era (1st to 3rd century AD).

You are the head of a **Sippe**, a Germanic kin group living between the Roman limes and the Amber Road. Run your household, grow your settlement, send expeditions into forest and moor, argue your case at the **Thing**, and trade with Rome.

Your most valuable resource is not gold or swords. It is **knowledge**. Whoever knows how to smelt iron, where the amber trail runs, or what the neighbouring patriarch is hiding shapes the world.

### What makes it different

- 🧠 **Knowledge is a world object.** It lives with people, spreads through conversation, gets distorted, dies with its holder, and can be traded, stolen or used as leverage.
- ⏳ **Generations in real time.** One in-game year is one real month. Your characters grow old, die, and pass their friendships, feuds and secrets on to their heirs.
- ⚖️ **Word before sword.** The Thing, guest right, wergild and marriage alliances are your main tools. Violence exists, but it is expensive.
- 🌲 **Raw, earthy, believable.** Historically grounded, with myth as perception. No horned helmets, no fantasy magic, no Viking clichés.
- 🌍 **One persistent world.** Never reset, shared by everyone, on mobile and desktop with one account.
- 🕰️ **Patience over panic.** Short, meaningful sessions. No paid timer skips, no punishment for being offline.

If *King of Dragon Pass*, *Crusader Kings* and *Travian* had a slow, social, knowledge-driven child, it would look a lot like this.

**Status:** in active development 🔨
